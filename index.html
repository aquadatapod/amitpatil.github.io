Research Focus

My research investigates how environmental drivers and emerging anthropogenic pressures shape biodiversity patterns across aquatic and semi-aquatic ecosystems. I specialize in uncovering macro-level patterns that govern species distribution, behavioral dynamics, and functional traits across global scales.[cite: 1]

A substantial component of my current portfolio examines the ecological implications of microplastics and emerging chemical variants cascading through trophic networks. By leveraging large datasets, global reviews, and multi-taxa frameworks, my work captures macroecological responses across diverse wildlife communities, spanning macroinvertebrates, shrimp, aquatic systems, and avian fauna.[cite: 1]

PhD Dissertation

My doctoral research extensively explored the structural and functional configurations of riverine macroinvertebrate communities within naturally occurring, repeated mesohabitats across the Wei River Basin (Shaanxi Province, Northwest China)[cite: 1]. The work established robust piecewise structural equation models (`piecewiseSEM`) to decouple complex environmental variables and isolate the core drivers shaping distribution arrays in river basins[cite: 1].
