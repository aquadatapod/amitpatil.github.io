Research

My research focuses on understanding how environmental change, human pressures, and ecological processes influence biodiversity and ecosystem functioning. I combine field ecology, ecological synthesis, species traits, and quantitative modelling to investigate ecological patterns across taxa, ecosystems, and spatial scales.

Research Themes

Macroecology & Biodiversity

Investigating broad-scale patterns in biodiversity, species traits, ecological interactions, and environmental responses across taxa and regions.

Aquatic & River Ecology

Studying aquatic communities and ecosystem processes across rivers, coastal systems, and other aquatic environments.

Pollution Ecology

Examining how microplastics, heavy metals, pesticides, and other emerging contaminants interact with organisms and ecological systems.

Ecological Data Science

Applying ecological modelling, statistical synthesis, functional traits, and large datasets to address questions in environmental biology.

Current Research

Microplastics & Wildlife

Investigating patterns of microplastic exposure and accumulation across wildlife, with particular emphasis on species traits, trophic ecology, body size, and environmental context.

Pollution & Aquatic Organisms

Exploring the ecological consequences of contaminants such as microplastics and heavy metals in aquatic organisms and coastal ecosystems.

Riverine Biodiversity & Ecosystem Functioning

Investigating how river geomorphology, environmental gradients, and mesohabitat structure influence macroinvertebrate communities and ecological functioning.

Global Ecological Synthesis

Integrating published datasets, species traits, ecological observations, and quantitative approaches to identify general ecological patterns across taxa and ecosystems.

Research Approach

  • Field-based ecological observation and sampling
  • Large-scale ecological data synthesis
  • Functional trait and community ecology
  • Statistical and hierarchical modelling
  • Structural equation modelling
  • Phylogenetic and comparative approaches
  • Data visualization and reproducible ecological analysis

Doctoral Research

My PhD research investigated the ecological functioning of riverine macroinvertebrate communities across different mesohabitats in the Wei River Basin, Shaanxi Province, Northwest China.
