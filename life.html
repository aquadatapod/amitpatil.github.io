Life Beyond Research

Research is a major part of my life, but curiosity does not stop at the laboratory or field site. This space is a collection of interests, experiences, and moments that connect with the way I see and explore the world.

Photography

Seeing the world through a camera

Photography is one of the ways I slow down and observe the details of landscapes, wildlife, people, and everyday environments. I particularly enjoy photographing natural environments and the small ecological stories that are often easy to overlook.

Nature & Exploration

Exploring natural environments

Time spent outdoors often provides a different perspective on ecology. Exploring forests, rivers, wetlands, coastlines, and other natural environments continues to shape my curiosity beyond formal research.

Underwater

Exploring beneath the surface

Diving offers another way of experiencing aquatic environments directly. Underwater landscapes, marine life, and the complexity of aquatic ecosystems are a continuing source of curiosity and inspiration.

Travel & Field Experiences

Places, people, and experiences

Research and travel have taken me to different environments and communities. I enjoy documenting these experiences, particularly the connections between landscapes, cultures, and the natural world.

A Space Outside Academia

This page will gradually grow with photographs, field memories, interesting places, and other experiences that have become part of my journey beyond academic work.
